# Privacy Policy for DailySpark

**Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

DailySpark ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the **DailySpark** mobile application.

---

### 1. Information Stored Locally
DailySpark is designed as a privacy-focused, local-first application:
- All of your personal app data—including saved favorite quotes, custom created quote card designs, streak progress, and daily achievements—is stored strictly on your local device storage.
- We do not operate external databases or servers that collect or store your saved quotes or personal journal data.

---

### 2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)
To keep DailySpark free for all users, we integrate **Google AdMob** (provided by Google LLC) to display banner and interstitial advertisements within the app.

**Information Collected by Google AdMob:**
- Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain technical data from your device, including:
  - Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / GAID)
  - IP address and general geographic location
  - App interaction data, ad engagement, and performance metrics
  - Diagnostic and crash logs related to ad delivery

**How Ad Data is Used:**
- AdMob uses this information to serve non-personalized or personalized ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and comply with advertising policies.
- You can manage your ad consent and personalization preferences at any time in the **DailySpark App Settings** or through your device's Android Google Settings (`Settings` > `Google` > `Ads`).

**Google's Privacy & Terms:**
- For more information on how Google collects and uses your data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy:
  👉 [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  👉 [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

### 3. Device Permissions
The app requests only the minimum device permissions required for core features:
- **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required to fetch AdMob advertisements and load soundscape streams.
- **Storage / Media Access:** Used strictly when you explicitly choose to save a custom-designed quote image to your device photo gallery.
- **Local Notifications:** Used to schedule your daily quote reminder alerts locally on your device.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
DailySpark is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. AdMob ads served in the app comply with Google Play Families and Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) guidelines.

---

### 5. Terms & User Rights
- You may uninstall the app at any time to clear all locally saved quote data and reset device advertising identifiers.
- If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our official store support page or GitHub repository.

---

### 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app enhancements or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date.
