# Privacy Policy for DailySpark

**Effective Date:** July 17, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at DailySpark. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the DailySpark mobile application.

### 1. Information We Collect
**We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.** 
DailySpark is designed to be a private, local-first application. All of your data, including your favorite quotes, customized app settings, and journal entries, are stored strictly locally on your device using standard offline storage mechanisms. We do not transmit, upload, or sync this data to any external servers or third-party databases. 

### 2. Device Permissions and Access
The app may request certain device permissions to provide core functionality:
- **Haptic Feedback:** Used to provide tactile responses when you interact with buttons or Zen Soundscapes.
- **Notifications:** Used to deliver your daily scheduled quotes or reminders. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device and are not triggered by a remote server.
- **Local Storage:** Used to save your personal preferences, favorites, and downloaded soundscapes.

None of these permissions are used to collect identifiable information.

### 3. Third-Party Services
We do not use any third-party tracking, analytics (such as Google Analytics), or advertising frameworks (such as AdMob) that secretly collect or harvest user data. Your usage of the app remains entirely private to you.

### 4. Data Security
Because your data never leaves your device, the security of your information is tied directly to the security of your own smartphone or tablet. We encourage you to protect your device with a strong passcode or biometric lock. If you uninstall the app or clear the app's cache, your saved data (favorites, journal entries) will be permanently deleted from your device.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect data from or market to children under 13 years of age. Since the app operates entirely offline and collects no data, it is inherently safe for users of all ages.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

### 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy or our practices, please feel free to reach out to the developer directly through our official GitHub repository.
