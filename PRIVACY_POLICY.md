# Privacy Policy for DailySpark

**Effective Date:** July 17, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at DailySpark. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

### 1. Information We Collect
**We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.** 
DailySpark is designed to be a private, local-first application. All of your data, including your favorite quotes and journal entries, are stored locally on your device using standard local storage mechanisms. We do not transmit this data to any external servers.

### 2. Permissions
The app may request certain permissions (such as haptic feedback or notifications) to provide core functionality. These permissions are strictly used on your device to enhance your experience and do not collect any identifiable information.

### 3. Third-Party Services
We do not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising frameworks that collect user data.

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
